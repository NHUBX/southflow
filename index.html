About this track

“මාතර කිඹුලා (NILWALA FLOW)” takes inspiration from the old Matara crocodile folk story and the name of Angamana Sumana. Traditional Southern Sri Lankan imagery meets drill drums, deep 808 bass, and crowd-ready chants.

This track features AI-generated vocals and AI-assisted music, created as a modern tribute to local folklore and culture.
